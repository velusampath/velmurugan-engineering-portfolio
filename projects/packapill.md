# Packapill

Public presentation page for the pharmacy delivery project highlighted in my resume.

## Project Summary

Packapill is a pharmacy delivery solution designed to connect customers with pharmacists and support medicine, prescription, and OTC product delivery through mobile and web channels. I contributed as a Team Lead, handling delivery coordination while supporting the technical implementation across frontend and backend layers. Across my Bthinq projects, I worked with .NET Core, Angular, Ionic, and ReactJS.

## My Role

- Company: Bthinq Information Systems Private Limited
- Role: Team Lead
- Duration: 01/2019 - 07/2024
- Location: Chennai, India

## Feature List

- Pharmacy delivery workflows for medicine and OTC products
- Support for prescription-based ordering flows
- Mobile and web experiences for customer ordering
- Operational coordination between customers, pharmacists, and delivery processes
- Delivery-focused planning, resource management, and project tracking support
