# Reading Doctor

Public presentation page for the literacy platform highlighted in my resume.

## Project Summary

Reading Doctor is a literacy-focused platform designed to improve phonics, reading, and spelling through web, mobile, and game-based experiences. My work covered frontend, mobile, interactive game, and backend delivery across the product. Across my Bthinq projects, I worked with .NET Core, Angular, Ionic, and ReactJS.
