# Tandym AI Recruitment Platform

Public presentation page for the Tandym AI recruitment project delivered at Moback Technologies.

## Project Summary

At Moback Technologies, I worked on the Tandym AI Recruitment Platform. My hands-on delivery focused on Python and .NET Core services for AI-powered resume parsing, candidate profile enrichment, vacancy-to-candidate matching, recruiter workflows, and secure multi-tenant backend APIs.

## My Role

- Company: Moback Technologies India Private Ltd
- Role: Team Lead / Senior Full Stack Developer
- Project: Tandym AI Recruitment Platform
- Duration: 08/2024 - Present
- Location: Bengaluru, India

## Feature List

- AI-powered resume and document ingestion from uploaded files
- Extraction of candidate skills, experience, education, and context from unstructured documents
- Vacancy-to-candidate matching with structured fitness reasons and classification scores
- Multi-tenant recruiter and candidate portal authentication
- Integration with Microsoft Dataverse / Dynamics 365 for candidate, client, and vacancy synchronization
- Reporting services for active positions, contractor statistics, and functional skill breakdowns
